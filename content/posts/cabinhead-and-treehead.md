## Design Interation

![](/treehead-generations.png)

A challenge of capturing existing characters in bricks is figuring out a scale that works for the character to be "read" as what it is designed to represent. Originally, I tried a smaller scale than the roughly 6 x 6 stud scale I ended up with but after a few attempts it was clear that to capture the wideset eyes I would need some space.

![](/treehead-mouth.png)

The second challenge is deciding on the "mouth pose". Depending on the scene the characters have silly sort of chins/lips or big grins. I took a few swings at capturing the lips but it wasn't reading right. So, we made the wide grins that appear on the cover- the end result is silly and charming.

## Color Selection

For this build I used GoBricks from the online vendor [BrickWith.com](https://brickwith.com). The primary reason for GoBricks is that the colors we ended up liking, Olive Green and Dark Orange, did not have parts available from the Dutch-brand. And frankly, sourcing parts with BrickWith is was easier than any other option.

The parts from BrickWith came packaged by model and I experienced zero missed parts in the few sets I audited.
